1. Launch the application using Streamlit:
   streamlit run app.py

2. Enter the meeting title in the input field.

3. Click "Start Recording" to begin capturing audio 
   OR upload a meeting audio file.

4. Speak into the microphone or play a meeting recording.

5. Click "Stop Recording" to end the session.

6. The system automatically processes:
   - Speech-to-Text conversion using Whisper
   - Speaker diarization using pyannote
   - Text summarization using BART model

7. View the outputs in the UI:
   - Real-time transcription log
   - Speaker-wise (diarized) transcript
   - Generated meeting summary

8. Use export options:
   - Download summary as Markdown
   - Download summary as PDF

9. Use email feature:
   - Enter sender email and app password
   - Enter recipient email
   - Click "Send Email" to share the summary

10. All meeting data is saved with timestamp in logs for future reference.
